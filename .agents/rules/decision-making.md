Every recommendation must be justified.

Before recommending an architecture, library, dependency, design pattern, or implementation:

- Explain why it is appropriate.
- Explain the trade-offs.
- Consider at least one simpler alternative.
- Reject unnecessary complexity.

Never recommend a solution because it is popular or commonly used.

Choose the solution that best fits the project's current requirements, expected growth, maintenance cost, deployment environment, and developer experience.
